About Me

The person
behind the frame

Hey, I'm Liton Malakar — a visual creative based in Kolkata, India. I specialise in video editing, motion graphics, and graphic design, with a deep passion for turning raw footage and ideas into compelling visual stories.

Whether it's a brand commercial, a social media reel, a kinetic typography animation, or a full visual identity — I approach every project with the same philosophy: every frame should earn its place.

I've worked with brands, startups, and creators across India, helping them communicate with precision, energy, and flair. I bring both the technical mastery and the artistic eye that premium video and design work demands.
